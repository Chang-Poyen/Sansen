Quite often many sources of mismatch are often overlooked. For example for a fully-differential
input stage of an opamp, good matching not only requires perfectly symmetrical layout but also
identical surroundings. For example, the substrate contacts of the input transistors must also
have the same size and an equal distance to the axis of symmetry. If not, different substrate
resistances result, and different body effects.
