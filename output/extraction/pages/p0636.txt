Voltage multipliers show
several more drawbacks.
They generate higher
voltage than the supply volt-
age. Care must be taken not
to exceed the maximum
voltage on the thin Gate
oxides.
At about 0.8 V/nm thick-
ness an oxide breaks down.
A CMOS technology of
0.18 mm has an oxide thick-
ness of about 1/50th or
3.6 nm. This would break
down under a voltage of
about 2.9 V. The standard
supply voltage for 0.18 mm CMOS is 1.8 V. This is sufficiently far from the breakdown voltage.
However, if voltage multiplies are added, care has to be take not to end up too close to the
breakdown voltage. If not, reliability issues appear.
A CMOS technology of 90 nm has an oxide thickness of about 1.8 nm, corresponding to a
breakdown voltage of about 1.5 V. The supply voltage is 1.3 V. Voltage multipliers are therefore
excluded!
