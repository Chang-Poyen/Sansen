Full feedforward means that
the input signal is fed
directly to the quantizer. As
a result, the noise transfer
function H (z) is the same as
e
in a conventional topology
but the signal transfer func-
tion H (z) is unity. This sug-
x
gests that the distortion is a
lot less in the full-feedfor-
ward topology.
Indeed, the signal goes
directly to the quantizer
without passing through the
loop filter integrators. These
filters only process the
quantization error, which is much smaller in amplitude than the signal itself. The distortion will
be a lot less indeed. The overload level can be higher and so is the dynamic range.
