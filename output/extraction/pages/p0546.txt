It is clear that at low frequencies the distortion of the output stage again dominates. The

At high frequencies, the distortion of the input stage takes over. The contributions of the
second stage in the middle are always negligible. This is a result of the compensation scheme in
which the lowest non-dominant pole is at a lower frequency than the one of the second stage.
It is clear that a number of less important non-linearities have been neglected. The most
important ones are the output conductances of the output transistors.
