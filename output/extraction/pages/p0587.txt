For sake of comparison, three realizations for the same application are listed. They all aim at
an IF filter for FM. The center frequency is 10.7 MHz and the passband is about 250 kHz. The
first is realized by a switched-capacitor techniques, the second uses RC components around
opamps and the third uses Gm blocks such as differential pairs, capacitors and tunable resistors.
It is clear that the first two realizations suffer from insufficient gain at this fairly high frequency.
