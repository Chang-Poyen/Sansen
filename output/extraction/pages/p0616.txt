Interpolation allows to reduce the number of preamplifiers and the input capacitance, the number
of latches being the same, however. Folding can be used to reduce the number of latches. Most
often interpolation is used in combination with folding to reduce both preamplifiers and latches,
which leads to a more drastic reduction in power consumption.
