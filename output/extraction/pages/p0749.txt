In the previous example,
a 7-stage ring oscillator
is used as a noise source
and only a single-CMOS
inverter-amplifier as receiver.
This has been extended to a
220-Kgates telecom chip as
a noise source and a WLAN
modem as receiver. The
bonding wires are 6 nH.
Both the peak-to-peak
and the RMS noise are
shown (on the left). The
largest peak around 75
MHz can also be found by
simulation. Accurate values
have been introduced for all parasitic resistances and capacitances.
There is a fairly linear relation between the noise and the supply voltage. This is a result of
the increased current injection in the substrate, which increases linearly with the supply voltage.
It is also found that as a great deal of noise is generated by the Input/Output buffers as by
the digital gates themselves!
