the stability of an opera-

amplifier, to be able to
implement it by means of

Now we would like to
refine the design plan. Up
till now we have arbitrarily
chosen the compensation
capacitances. The question
is, how can we improve on
that?

focused on a few specifica-
tions only, which are the GBW and the Phase Margin. Surely there are many more specifications.
