This voltage-to-current conversion is even more accurate if a full operational amplifier is
inserted in the feedback loop, instead of just one single transistor. The loop gain now includes
the open-loop gain of the operational amplifier. The distortion will now be very small.
This is only true at low frequencies, however, as the open-loop gain of an opamp drops quite
rapidly versus frequency. For high frequencies the circuit on the left may be preferable.
