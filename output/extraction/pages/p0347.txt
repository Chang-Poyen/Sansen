The addition of just one single transistor changes the operation drastically and converts this
stage into a class-AB amplifier. Transistor M4B is added, which forms a current mirror with
M4A as well. It provides current feedback to the differential pair.

M2A, M1A and M4B. The other one flows through M2B, M3 and M4A, and is multiplied with
