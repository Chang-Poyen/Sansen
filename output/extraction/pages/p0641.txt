2131
The first remedy is to make everything fully differential. A fully-differential opamp is now used,
as shown in this slide.
It is a two-stage Miller opamp with a folded cascode as a first stage. A second stage is needed
to provide rail-to-rail output swing. The gain can be quite high, 75 dB in this example.
For a 1 pF load, the GBW is about 30 MHz, for only 80 mW power consumption. This gives
a high FOM indeed!
The opamp can be switched in and out because of the four switches (in blue). Only the output
stage is switched in and out, by transistors M11. The input stage remains on, which improves
the settling time.
