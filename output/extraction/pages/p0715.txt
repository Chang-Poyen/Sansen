is used to improve the isola-

input. This is especially
required to avoid leakage
from the next block, which
is usually a mixer, to the
input of the LNA.
In order to avoid noise
coming from the cascode, its
DC current is reduced.
A current source with
pMOST, provides some of
the DC current to the input
transistor. In this way, the
contribution of the noise of the load devices can be reduced somewhat.
