Note that the input sees four differential pairs in parallel. This is why folding does not decrease
the input capacitance. Each folding block has as many differential pairs in parallel as the folding
rate indicates. The input capacitance is the same as for a flash ADC.
Note also that the output frequency of a folding block is at a higher frequency than the input
voltage. This establishes the maximum frequency of operation.
