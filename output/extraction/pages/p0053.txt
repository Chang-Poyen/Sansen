The product of the low-frequency gain and the bandwidth is called the Gain-Bandwidth
product GBW. It is by far the most important quality factor of an amplifier. Actually it is the
most important specification of an amplifier. Later on we will compare amplifiers by means of
a Figure of Merit (FOM), which quotes how much GBW can be obtained for a certain load
capacitance and power consumption.
The GBW itself is readily obtained. It only depends on the transistor transconductance and
the load capacitance, not on the output resistance. We will see that this expression is valid for
all possible single-stage amplifiers. It is therefore a very important expression!
