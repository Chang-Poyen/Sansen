The configurations in this
slide exploit the square-law
relationship of a MOST in
strong inversion, to extract
a doubling of the transcon-
ductance as a result of a
multiplication of the current
by four.
At lower currents, and at
lower frequencies, weak
inversion can be used. In
this case, the transcon-
ductance is proportional
to the current and current
feedback loops can be
used to equalize the trans-
conductance.
1124
In weak inversion, the trans-
conductance is proportional
to the current. Maintaining
a constant current over
the whole common-mode
input range will provide
a constant total trans-
conductance.
There is one problem
however. The factor n is
different for nMOST and
pMOST devices. Remember
that this n factor is not very
precise because it contains
the depletion layer capaci-
tance, which depends on
biasing voltages.
Nevertheless, it is vital to try to compensate for this difference in the n factor. An error in
transconductance will otherwise result.
