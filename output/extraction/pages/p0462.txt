The operational amplifier is a folded cascode, with M1/M2 as input devices and the Drains
of M3B/M4B (point PDII) as output. They operate in weak inversion and are now quite large
to suppress offset and 1/f noise. It is a two-stage amplifier. A compensation capacitance is
therefore required.
The startup circuit is on the left. It includes a Power-Down function. The Drain of transistor
M7 (point PDII) biases the amplifier and the bandgap reference. There are two inverters between
this point and PD, by means of transistors M8/M9 and M6/M7. When PD is high. The output
of the first inverter is low (point PDI) and the output of the next inverter is high (point PDII).
All pMOSTs connected at this point are thus off and so is the opamp and bandgap. The output
is low.
