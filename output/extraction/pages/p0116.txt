Let us now determine which
noise sources we can find in
an electronic circuit.
Resistors and junctions give
noise. We will take resis-
tors first.
A resistor gives thermal
noise. It is modeled by a
voltage source in series with
the resistor or a current
source in parallel.
The noise voltage is pro-
portional to that resistor,
and to the absolute temper-
ature (in Kelvin). It does not
depend on the actual cur-
rent flowing through the resistor. Cooling down will therefore reduce the noise (as in many space
applications!).
For a resistor of 1 kV, the thermal noise density is about 4 nV /√Hz at room temperature.
