Curvature correction is
always possible, if needed.
It is always based on tak-
ing two references with a
different curvature. We have
seen before that the curva-
ture for a current which is
independent of temperature
(m=0) is larger than for
a PTAT current (m=1).
The ratio of the correspond-
ing nonlinearity is about
g/(g−1). This ratio is actu-
ally independent of the cur-
rent levels used.
A weighted addition of
the output voltages then allows perfect compensation of the curvature. Curvature compensation
