An integrated current
amplifier is depicted in this
slide.
It is a two-stage amplifier
with current input. The
output of the first stage is at
the Drains of transistors
Q21/Q22. Transistors Q1
and Q2 now serve as emitter
followers to drive the
second stage.
In this stage, pnp-npn
composites are used to drive
the output load. For exam-
ple, the Q3/Q5 composite
behaves as a super-pnp tran-
sistor. On the other side,
Q4/Q6 are a super npn.
The compensation capacitances CC1 and CC2 are connected from the middle point of such a
composite, which is a strange point indeed.
The performance is great: 110 MHz bandwidth and 230 V/ms, using bipolar transistors with
an f of 3.8 GHz only!
