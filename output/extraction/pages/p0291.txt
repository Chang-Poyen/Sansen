The input cascode transistors determine the noise performance in single-stage current amplifi-
ers. Active loads are now used for these cascodes. Is their noise still dominant?
The answer is positive. Indeed, at higher frequencies, the compensation capacitance acts as a
short-circuit. The second stage is little more than an impedance 1/g . This is a low impedance.
