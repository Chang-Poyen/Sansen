A much better behavior
is achieved by taking an
electronic Zener. For this
purpose, an amplifier is
added in parallel with the
nMOST Zener diode fol-
lowed by a source follower.
The transitions are now
much better defined. This
means that the error in
transconductance over the
common-mode input range
is smaller.

However, the error in trans-
conductance is only 6% in
contrast with 25% without
the amplifier.
The larger current in the
input devices leads to a
larger Slew Rate, which is
a considerable additional
advantage.
