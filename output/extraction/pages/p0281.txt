The first category uses no
compensation capacitance. It is clear that this is not the way to go!
The second category is the most used one. It lists all the NMC varieties. Several of them have
been discussed in this Chapter. The FOMs are reasonably good. The best one is the multi-path
opamp MNMC. However, its Slew Rate is not so good, because no feedforward stage is used to
turn the output stage into a class-AB stage.

increase the FOM a lot.
