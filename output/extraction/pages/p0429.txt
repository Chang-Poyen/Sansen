Mismatch is determined
mainly by size and a number
of other design rules, which

It is already known that
matching improves with the
square root of the size. This
is the main criterion. It
always works. There are
many other layout condi-
tions however, which help in
the reduction of mismatch.
They all work to some
extent, even when proof is
not easy to find in the litera-
ture. They are summarized
in 10 rules, as shown next.
