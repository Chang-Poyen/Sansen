A few simple startup circuits are shown in this slide.
A capacitance at the base of the pnp current mirror draws a current when the supply voltage
is switched on. This current flows through the pnp transistors and starts injecting a current in
the bottom npn transistors as well, biasing up the bandgap reference circuit.
However, if for some other reason the current drops to zero, then the supply voltage has to
be switched on and off again.
