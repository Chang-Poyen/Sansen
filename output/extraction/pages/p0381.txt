An introduction on feed-
back has been given in the
previous Chapter design.
The four basic types of feed-
back have been identified.
Two of them have been
discussed in the previous
Chapter. They are the types
with series feedback at the
input as they measure a
voltage.
In this Chapter, we will
focus on the two other types
of feedback, i.e. the ones
with shunt feedback at the
input. As a consequence,
they measure an input current. They are therefore transimpedance amplifiers, if an output voltage
is provided, or current amplifiers.
