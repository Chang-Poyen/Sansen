As a result, a fully integratable low-pass filter can be realized at low frequencies. There are
only two drawbacks. It can only function at signal frequencies much lower than the clock
frequency. Moreover the ratio of the signal frequency to the clock frequency is determined by a
capacitor ratio. Large values of this ratio are not easy to realize. The signal frequency cannot
thus allowed to be too large but not too small either!
