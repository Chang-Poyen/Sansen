In this Chapter, an overview
is given of the techniques
which are available to
reduce the supply voltage
and the power consumption
in sigma-delta converters.
The main problem at low
supply voltages is the input
sampling switch and the
increase of the distortion.
This can be remedied by the
switched-opamp technique
and also by a number of
clever circuit techniques.
They have all been dis-
cussed and compared for a
generally accepted FOM.
