The following test pro-

The stress test is executed between two pins (here pin1 and pin 2). First of all, the leakage
current is measured between these two pins. Then a high voltage discharge is applied after which
the leakage current is measured again. The discharge voltage is stepped up until the leakage
current has become excessive. An example of such a TLP test is given at the end of this Chapter.
