The AGC loop works as follows. When no signal is present, the Gate voltage of M5 is fairly
low and a large current is sent to M1. When the oscillation has come up, more current is flowing
through M3 such that the gate of M5 increases. As a result, the current in M3 decreases and so
does the current in M1. In equilibrium, the AGC circuit maintains the minimum current for
which oscillation is sustained, which corresponds with point A.
