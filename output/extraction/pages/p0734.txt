Do all digital gates cause
current spikes? The answer
is negative. Current spikes
are typical for CMOS logic.
No current is consumed in
either the zero output state
or the output state. Current
is only consumed during
the transition. This is the
strength of CMOS logic.
The static power consump-
tion is very low but
increases with the clock
frequency.
Several other loc families
exist which consume current
continuously. ECL in bipolar technology or current-mode logic in CMOS (see slide) are good
examples. As current is consumed continuously, the power consumption is high. The advantage
however, is that the current spikes are hardly visible.
