To obtain more gain,

This feedback is actually

up by the same amount.
If we realize that feedback amplifier by just one transistor M3, the gain of that transistor is
added to the total gain.
An additional advantage of that feedback amplifier is that the impedance in the middle (at
the gate of M3) is reduced by the same feedback gain.
