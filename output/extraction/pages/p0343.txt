A three-fold translinear loop is now formed. Two of them include the output transistors. They
are M2/M12 and M4/M11. They are added towards current mirror M9/M7. The third one is
M15/M13+14. They are added towards current mirror M10/M8.
The purpose of these loops is to prevent one output transistor to go off, when the other one
is carrying a large current. Indeed when transistor M2 provides a large output current, M1 may
shut off. Even for large output currents, a minimum current through M1 must be guaranteed.
This decreases the cross-over distortion and increases the speed.
If the current through M1 is very small, the currents through M3 and M4 are also small. As
a result, V becomes larger. For a large current in M2, V becomes smaller. The product
