In this Chapter, an intro-
duction is given on distor-
tion caused by the weak
nonlinearities of the transis-
tors. The definitions of dis-
tortion have been given first.
They are followed by distor-
tion calculations of MOST
and bipolar devices in both
single and differential con-
figurations.
Considerable attention is
paid to the reduction of dis-
tortion by feedback. Finally
some guide lines and over-
view Tables are added to
allow easy prediction of dis-
tortion levels in any kind of
amplifier.
