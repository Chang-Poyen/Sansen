We will not discuss the
effects of linear distortion in
this Chapter but of nonlin-
ear distortion.
Linear distortion is a
result of filter action. A per-
fectly linear system which
shows some kind of filtering
action, will distort the pic-
ture of the signal versus
time. For example, a high-
pass filter will emphasize the
steep edges of a square
waveform, as they represent
the higher frequencies. The
output waveform has there-
fore peaked. It is different from the input waveform, and is distorted. This is linear distortion. It
occurs for all filters.
