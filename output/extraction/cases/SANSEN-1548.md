## 对应教材讲解

### PDF 437 · 书本 445

A nice way to avoid rounding of corners is to make round transistor shapes. Surrounding the Drain of a MOST with its Gate, and adding a Source all around has been known to yield excellent matching. Unfortunately, not all layout systems allow such round shapes. An orthogonal or hexagonal shape is not that good!
